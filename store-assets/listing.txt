Invisibles: Find & Remove Hidden Characters (AI text cleaner)

Find the characters you cannot see, then remove them. Paste an AI reply, a link, a spreadsheet cell, or a diff. Invisibles names every hidden character, warns when a letter or a hostname only looks familiar, and flags bidirectional controls that can make code display differently from how it runs.

It runs in the side panel, on your device.

What it finds
- Zero-width spaces, joiners, the word joiner, the byte-order mark, Hangul filler, and other blank characters
- No-break spaces and other look-alike spaces, including the narrow no-break space reported in some chatbot replies
- Bidirectional controls (Trojan Source), including on lines you paste from a diff
- Soft hyphens, tag characters, variation selectors, and other non-printing controls
- Hidden text carried by tag characters or variation selectors, decoded into plain words
- Look-alike letters in a Latin word, and hostnames that resemble a different name (shown with the DNS form)
- Visible punctuation people associate with chatbot paste — em dashes, smart quotes, ellipsis — counted separately, because it is not a hidden character and not proof of who wrote the text

What you can do
- See where each hidden character sits, and skip a category when you clean
- Remove hidden characters, or turn look-alike spaces into normal spaces. Emoji stay whole
- Clean on paste, and clean on copy from the box. Both stay off until you turn them on
- Clean AI paste: hidden characters plus plain-ASCII punctuation, in one step
- A toolbar badge with the current count, and a tally of how many hidden characters you have cleaned on this browser. The tally is a number. Your text is not stored
- A suggested shortcut, Ctrl+Shift+. (Command+Shift+. on Mac), opens the panel. Change it in Chrome’s extension shortcuts

What it does not do
Invisibles identifies characters, not authorship or intent. It does not read a statistical watermark, and it does not read the pages you visit. Look-alike letters are warnings, not silent rewrites. Some hidden characters are legitimate, so review the findings before cleaning.

Privacy and access
- No host access: the extension cannot read the pages you visit.
- No account, analytics, advertising, or remote service.
- Pasted text stays in the side panel, is processed only in memory, and is never stored or transmitted.
- Permissions: sidePanel (open the tool beside the current tab) and storage (remember UI preferences and a count of characters cleaned — not your pasted text).
